# Medialane on Starknet

**The Medialane launchpad and marketplace, with your own wallet.**

[starknet.medialane.io](https://starknet.medialane.io) is the Medialane app for people who already live onchain. Connect the Starknet wallet you use today and create, launch, collect and trade programmable IP with full wallet sovereignty.

New to Starknet? [medialane.io](https://medialane.io) gets you started with just an email and a passkey-secured wallet. Both apps share one catalog, so your work and your collection are the same wherever you open them.

---

## Bring your own wallet

Ready (Argent), Braavos, Cartridge Controller, MetaMask, Keplr, Fordefi and Xverse. Every action is signed by you, in your wallet.

---

## Creator Launchpad

- **Single editions and collections:** publish a work once, inside a collection you own.
- **Limited editions:** release a work in numbered copies.
- **Collection Drops:** timed releases with your price and supply.
- **Remix:** licensed derivatives with credit and royalties flowing back to the original.
- **Creator Coins:** launch a coin with a public trading pool, or claim a coin you already launched.
- **POP:** permanent, non-transferable badges for events, communities and milestones.
- **IP Tickets:** verifiable tickets for events and access.
- **IP Club:** membership cards with tiers and validity windows.
- **IP Sponsorship:** sponsors bid for a license on your work, settled directly between you.

## Coins

Discover creator coins and memecoins, with live prices read directly from each coin's public pool, and trade them from your wallet.

## Marketplace

- List, buy, make offers and accept offers.
- Check out a whole cart at once.
- Full provenance and license on every asset.

## Creator pages and claims

- A creator page at your wallet address or at a username you claim.
- Collections, listings, analytics and activity in one profile.
- Claim a collection you deployed, a clean collection URL, or your Genesis NFT.

## Development

```bash
bun install
cp .env.example .env.local
bun dev
```

Before opening a pull request, run `bun run typecheck`, `bun run lint` and `bun test`.
